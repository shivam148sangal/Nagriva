# Nagriva 🌐

### AI-Powered Citizen Grievance & Smart Governance Platform

> **Your Voice. The Right Authority. Real Action.**

Nagriva is a prototype that connects citizens with the right government authority to report and resolve civic issues in both **rural and urban communities**.

Citizens can report problems using **voice, text, images, and location**. Nagriva uses AI to understand complaints, identify their priority, and help route them to the appropriate local authority or government department.

The platform demonstrates a complete grievance workflow with **real-time complaint tracking, GIS intelligence, SLA monitoring, citizen verification, government portals, and data analytics**.

---
